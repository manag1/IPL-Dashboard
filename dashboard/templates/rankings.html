WHAT DRIVES THE RATING?

IPL Intelligence Ranking Methodology

Transparent ICC-inspired methodology used by this dashboard.

Player ratings

Every match produces a contextual performance score. That score is blended with the player's previous rating using a weighted moving average, so recent form matters more while established performance is retained.

  • Batters: runs, strike rate, scoring difficulty, not-out status, quality of bowlers faced, quality of bowlers against whom runs were scored, result and knockout context.
  • Bowlers: wickets, economy, workload, scoring difficulty, ratings of batters dismissed, opposition batting strength, result and knockout context.
  • All-rounders: batting and bowling ratings combined using the ICC-style multiplicative index.
  • Teams: match result, opposition strength, margin and IPL title context are combined. The winner of the last match of each season receives championship context.

Qualification

Players need a minimum match sample before they are shown in the primary leaderboard. New players receive a sample-size dampening factor so one exceptional match cannot immediately create an unrealistic rating.

Knockout context

The current database does not store playoff labels. For full IPL seasons, the final four chronological matches are treated as knockout matches for ranking context.
